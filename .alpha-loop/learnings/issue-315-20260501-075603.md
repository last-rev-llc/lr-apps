## Patterns
- Ordered cleanup with non-blocking secondary operation: delete authoritative row first, then attempt blob cleanup; on blob failure, log structured warning (`feature`, `phase`) for a sweep cron rather than rolling back. Lets the user-facing operation succeed even when storage is flaky.
- In-memory query builder mock pattern: track `pendingUpdate`/`pendingDelete`/`pendingInsert` flags inside the chained builder so `.then()` and `.single()` can dispatch to the right handler — scales to multiple mutation actions without per-test setup.
