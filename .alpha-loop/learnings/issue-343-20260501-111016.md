## Patterns
- Mock `@repo/db/server` at module level with `vi.mock` and have the mocked `from().insert/update/delete` return the row shape; assertions on `revalidatePath` and span name (`crm.<action>`) validate instrumentation contracts.
- When promoting/removing a sub-app, delete its `lib/queries.ts` + `page.tsx` body and replace `page.tsx` with a `redirect()` stub, then remove the registry/MODULES entry and update the corresponding command-center page.test.tsx EXPECTED_MODULES list in the same commit.
